How It Works
  1. Customer calls J. Austin → Tark1 (voice AI on 192.168.1.200) answers
  2. Tark1 detects what the customer wants (scrap gold, silver eagles, etc.)
  3. Tark1 calls GET /api/toggles/ on Frontdesk to check this board
  4. Toggle ON: Tark1 collects name/phone/time preference and calls POST /api/voice-schedule/ to book the appointment automatically
  5. Toggle OFF: Tark1 tells the caller "we'll call you back shortly"
  6. Item not on board: Tark1 says "let me have someone call you back about that"
